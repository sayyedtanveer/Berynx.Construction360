### Business rules

- **BR-M03-01:** Duplicate matches use GSTIN, PAN, mobile, or normalized name+city; merge is audited, preserves merge lineage, relinks current M03 references, and rejects overlapping customer price-list periods.
- **BR-M03-02–03:** `M03CreditEvaluationService` composes tenant-scoped customer profile and transaction-facts providers. It evaluates manual hold, limit, credit days, overdue policy, confirmed-undelivered order value and unallocated advances; unknown facts fail closed and never become zero. Override requests require `credit.override`, actual available facts, an M02 approval request and an immutable audit snapshot. M04/M06/M08 must invoke the service at their decision points.
- **BR-M03-04 and BR-M03-10:** Effective-dated tax resolution validates supplied GSTIN/POS identity. `M03TaxSnapshot` is an immutable value contract that validates GSTIN checksums, place of supply, total rate and intra/inter-state components. Future posting modules persist their own snapshot; M03 never creates or edits invoices.
- **BR-M03-05–06:** Unit conversion normalization and reference checks are enforced; referenced masters are deactivated, not deleted.
- **BR-M03-07:** Price lookup is customer-specific (including the assigned customer list), then group, then default. Missing applicable product rates fail closed; below-minimum pricing submits an M02 approval request.
- **BR-M03-08:** Fleet expiry query includes expired and upcoming insurance/PUC/fitness/licence dates with 7/15/30-day urgency; vehicle, driver and equipment eligibility endpoints are wired. Refresh creates idempotent M02 in-app notifications with audit records. External email/SMS/WhatsApp delivery remains unverified.
- **BR-M03-09:** RMC rate-card exact-key lookup returns a blocking not-found response; admixture/pump surcharge references must be active configured charge types.
