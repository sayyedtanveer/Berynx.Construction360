# P1-M09 Delivery Challans — implementation checkpoint

**Status: partial module implementation.** M09 creates a unique challan from persisted M08 delivery facts and now participates in the authorized supervisor-review flow. Unverified/rejected deliveries remain `Unverified` and are not invoice-eligible. No database-backed acceptance or UAT is claimed.

## Implemented source

- One tenant/trip challan is protected by the existing unique key and created in the M08 completion transaction using the tenant challan series/counter.
- Challan snapshots include order/customer/site/product, delivered quantities, vehicle/driver/plant and receiver/evidence facts. No purchase costs are exposed.
- M08 Verify/Reject/resubmission recalculates the linked challan from persisted trip facts in the same unit of work. The status remains `Unverified` until the M08 authorized review records `Verified`; duplicate or invalid state transitions fail closed.
- M10 draft eligibility and posting re-read the trip and challan and compare the receiver name, evidence references and verification timestamp, rather than trusting stale challan status.
- Tenant/branch-scoped paging, detail/history, printable preview, Angular worklists, and existing audit/uniqueness behavior remain.

The Phase 1 decision excludes OTP/SMS. The M18 secure attachment adapter is not operational; the verifier refuses evidence until the storage/scanning contract confirms tenant, purpose, scan status, and secure preview. See `docs/modules/P1-M08.md`.
