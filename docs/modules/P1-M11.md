## Critical-chain implementation update — 2026-11-26

**Status: PARTIAL.** Receipt draft and post routes are wired to Angular and the existing M12 posting engine. Posting verifies draft version, customer/branch-consistent allocations to posted invoices, remaining invoice balances, receipt/allocation reconciliation, supported payment method and period controls; resolves active branch/GSTIN/effective-date mappings; uses the existing transactional voucher engine; and records receipt voucher linkage, actor/time and posting audit. Journal legs cover net cash/bank, TDS receivable and bank charges against invoice receivable and customer advances. Creation idempotency now compares material receipt fields and allocations. The open receivables view derives outstanding from posted allocations rather than a second balance calculation.

Posting is enabled only for Cash, Bank transfer, and UPI. Cheques/PDC remain blocked until clearance workflow exists; adjustments remain blocked pending approval. Refunds, retained amounts, advance-GST, credit/debit notes, part-paid/paid lifecycle, and bank reconciliation remain unimplemented. No database-backed posting/tie-out test ran.
